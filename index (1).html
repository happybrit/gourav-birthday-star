VEGA

ALPHA LYRAE · HD 172167 · HIP 91262

Among the countless lights in our sky, Vega feels wonderfully fitting: its light travels for about 25 years before reaching Earth. You turn 25 today, and that coincidence gives this star a place in your birthday story.

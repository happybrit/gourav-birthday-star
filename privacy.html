A Light for Gourav ✦

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. About this experience

A Light for Gourav is a personal birthday-gift experience available through the Telegram bot @StarlitGroveBot and the birthday website. The bot's menu opens the website. No registration is required to view the gift.

2. Information we use

The gift website does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, location, or payment details. It does not intentionally send personal information to the gift creator or run custom analytics or advertising trackers.

If you interact with the Telegram bot, Telegram handles information such as your account details and messages under Telegram's Privacy Policy. The current gift experience does not intentionally save bot conversations in a separate database. This policy will be updated before introducing features that collect or store additional data.

3. Theme preference on your device

The website may use your browser's local storage to remember your chosen appearance (blue-and-gold, nature-and-gold, or purple-and-gold). This preference stays in your browser and is not used to identify you. You can clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

4. Hosting and third-party websites

  • GitHub Pages hosts the birthday website. GitHub may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own privacy statement.
  • Stellarium Web opens the interactive view of the real star Vega when you choose Watch Vega.
  • SIMBAD provides an external scientific record for Vega.

When you choose an external link, the destination may process data according to its own privacy practices. We do not control those websites.

5. Sharing, advertising, and retention

We do not intentionally sell personal information, run advertisements, or build marketing profiles. The gift website itself does not maintain an account database or an independent visitor-history database. Browser preferences remain on your device until you clear them. Third-party providers apply their own retention rules.

6. Changes to this policy

If the bot or website later adds functionality that changes how information is processed, this notice will be revised to reflect the new behavior.

7. Questions

For questions about this project's privacy practices, contact the maintainer through the project's GitHub repository. Do not post private or sensitive information in public GitHub issues.
